# ivolopes.com

The hub: the one place that links to every area of ivolopes.com. The areas
themselves deliberately link to nothing else (each is its own space); the
shared contact is mail@ivolopes.com.

| Area | Address | Repo |
|---|---|---|
| QA (CV) | qa.ivolopes.com | githubdoivo/qa.ivolopes.com |
| Art (ceramics) | art.ivolopes.com | githubdoivo/art.ivolopes.com |
| MOSS (DJ, producer) | moss.ivolopes.com | githubdoivo/moss.ivolopes.com |
| Stage (dance, acting) | stage.ivolopes.com | githubdoivo/stage.ivolopes.com |

`index.html` is the solar system: the sun is the Contact link (mail), and the
planets are the areas. Images in `assets/` come from a ChatGPT mockup (sky,
plus planets cut out of a sprite sheet with circular masks).
Earlier design mocks live in `mocks/` on the Mac only (git-ignored, not published).
